Typography

A dual-font strategy. We use Manrope to deliver our bold brand voice, while Inter handles the precision required for utility data. Tight tracking on headlines creates an authoritative presence.

Color Harmony

Breathable and expensive. Our palette avoids harsh borders, favoring tonal shifts and atmospheric gradients.

The "No-Line" Rule

Layout boundaries are established exclusively through background color shifts. Use surface-container-low for secondary sections. The contrast is felt, not seen.

Glassmorphism

Overlays use surface-variant at 60% opacity with a 24px backdrop blur to create a premium "frosted glass" effect.

Building the Future of Aura

This system is designed to grow. As we expand the ecosystem, the core principles of Luminous Depth will ensure every touchpoint feels unmistakably premium.
